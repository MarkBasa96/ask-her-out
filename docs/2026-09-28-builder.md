## Decisions worth knowing

- **Mark's page stays byte-for-byte the same in behaviour.** The loader only runs with `/p/<slug>`, `?p=<slug>` or `?preview=1`. With none of them there's no `await`, no Supabase call, and the same Formspree flow. The only visible change to his page is the new logo (which he asked for).
- **No extra `<span>`s in his HTML.** The first try wrapped "love" and "boyfriend" in spans, but the Yes button and badge are flex containers with `gap`, which added space around them. The custom wording is now set with JavaScript on builder pages only, and his page was pixel-compared against the previous version (identical).
- **Pronouns.** His page says "he". Builder pages use the creator's **name** instead ("Jamie's still crazy about you"), because "they" needs different verbs. Role (boyfriend/girlfriend/partner) only changes "your man"/"your girl"/the name and the badge.
- **Answers store `starts_at` (UTC)** plus the day, time and timezone they picked. The dashboard shows the time in *their* timezone and builds Google Calendar/.ics links from real UTC stamps that keep the `Z`.
- **The anon role can't read `owner_id`** (column grants), and can't read answers or reports at all.
- **Hiding is admin-only**, enforced by a trigger: owners can't hide or unhide, even their own page. Admins are listed by email in `public.admins`, which has RLS on and no policies, so it can't be read or changed through the API.
- **The date page talks to Supabase with plain `fetch`** (PostgREST), so it doesn't load supabase-js. The builder and dashboard load supabase-js 2 from jsDelivr (`dist/umd/supabase.js`; there's no `.min.js` in current versions).
- **Photos are shrunk in the browser** (longest side 1200px, JPEG), which keeps them under 2 MB and strips location data.
- **Live preview** is the real `ask-her-out.html?preview=1` in an iframe. It reads the draft from `localStorage['dp-preview']`, loads no music, and never saves answers.
