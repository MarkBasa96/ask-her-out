# 2026-09-28: the rebuild

A record of what was asked for, what was decided and what was built in one Claude Code cloud session, so later sessions don't have to rediscover it.

## Starting point

The original page (Aug 2026) was a white card on a gradient night sky. It had a photo, Yes/No buttons, a No button that dodged the mouse within 100px, a "pretty please" dog photo, a date/time/email form, Formspree, an auto-opening Google Calendar tab, and YouTube music. The wording was "Will you go on a date with me?".

## Bugs found in the original

1. **Wrong calendar time.** Start/end came from `toISOString()` with the `Z` stripped, so Google read UTC times as local: 7 PM in Manila became 11 AM. Reproduced with `TZ=Asia/Manila`.
2. **Date a day early.** `new Date('2026-10-10')` parses as UTC, so it showed "Friday, October 9" in America/New_York.
3. **No button on phones.** The No button only reacted to `mousemove`, so on phones it could just be tapped.
4. **Formspree unchecked.** The POST had no `Accept: application/json` and never checked the response.
5. **Loose form.** Past dates were allowed and the email wasn't validated.
6. **Effects.** Confetti spawned at random spots and never fell; hearts and petals spawned in a single row.
7. **Photo.** White bars were baked into the main photo.
8. **Leftovers.** Lots of debug `console.log`s.

All fixed in the rebuild.

## How the design was decided

1. **First mockup:** a "paper objects on a night sky" look (envelope, Polaroid, ticket).
2. **Mark's reference:** a Pinterest link (blocked in the cloud environment) and then a screen recording of a dark "Redsun" AI landing page: black background, glowing orange eclipse arc, frosted-glass navbar, cards and pop-ups.
3. **His choices:**
   - colours: **sunset blend**, black background with the arc glowing orange into the original pink;
   - structure: **the video's look on the simple flow**, not a long spoof landing page;
   - name: **Mark**;
   - extras: left to Claude (cursor glow, tap hearts, rising sunrise, glass pop-ups).
4. **The sky:** he asked for it to feel magical and moving, with stars, shooting stars and comets. An animated preview was built and recorded as a video, and he approved it as is.
5. **New images:** a new photo of him in a black coat (to present him as "the hopeless romantic") and four cat pictures to replace the dog and Mingming.
6. **The pixel cat:** he asked for a pixel cat that walks everywhere, sometimes behind and sometimes in front of the text, pauses at random, and says sweet things in speech bubbles as if talking to his girlfriend.
7. **The wording:** she is already his girlfriend, so the wording was rewritten ("Can I steal you for a date, love?", "Yes, my love →", "You already have my heart. Now I'd like your calendar.").
8. **The No button:** he asked that it **never give up**. An earlier version got "tired" after 9 dodges; that was removed.
9. **The music:** he asked for it to autoplay when the page opens and keep playing, with a small mute icon. He mentioned "these subtle songs" but no song was attached, so the original song is still used. **Ask him for YouTube links if he wants different or multiple songs.**
